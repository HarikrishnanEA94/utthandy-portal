Utthandy teaches cybersecurity through short lessons, quizzes and hands-on challenges. Everything runs in your browser, and there's no account to create.

## Pick a path

- **New to security?** Start with **IT Security → Foundations**, then **Security Awareness**.
- **Working in IT?** Jump into the track that matches your role: networking, web, cloud or SOC.
- **Working in industrial or critical infrastructure?** Begin with **OT / ICS Security → OT Fundamentals**.

## Your progress

Your progress is saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent to a server. You'll be able to export it and import it on another device.
