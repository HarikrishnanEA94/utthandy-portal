---
title: About Utthandy
description: What Utthandy is and how it is built.
---

Utthandy is a free, open cybersecurity training portal. It focuses mainly on IT security and also covers operational technology (OT) and industrial control systems (ICS).

- **Open source.** Lessons are Markdown files on [GitHub](https://github.com/HarikrishnanEA94/utthandy-portal), and corrections are welcome.
- **Private by design.** There are no accounts and no tracking cookies. Your progress stays in your browser.
- **Grounded in primary sources.** Lessons cite NIST, CISA, OWASP, MITRE and IEC wherever possible.
