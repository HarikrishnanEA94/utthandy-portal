## Rules of engagement

Utthandy teaches offensive techniques so you can defend against them. **Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to test.** Unauthorised access is illegal in most countries. Examples include the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (US), the Computer Misuse Act (UK) and the IT Act 2000 (India).

## Privacy

This site doesn't use accounts or tracking cookies. Learning progress is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.
